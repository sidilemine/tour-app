## Practical blockers, limitations and decisions

**Headset feedback capture is unresolved.** A failed recording can lose valuable spoken feedback even when the file saves. The [same-session native comparison](M2-next-technical-session.md) is prepared; no causal native fix is supported yet. Phone microphone is the owner-observed music-interruption fallback, held near the mouth with Review visible. Its current quality has not been separately confirmed. For the next walking/listening version, private recording need not block the tour if the known fallback or text notes are sufficient; it must not be advertised as a working Bluetooth recording path.

**Audible review-close continuation remains a small device question.** Existing tests and cold-open installation support the new implementation, not audible behavior. One brief active-story review/close is the useful next observation. Ended-tour preservation, automatic-off and freshness cases have automated coverage; they do not all need another physical matrix.

**Direction clarity and automatic use remain distinct M2 gaps.** The Crescent instruction is a known misleading approach, not a hypothetical edge case; examine and resolve it before reusing it for an unfamiliar visitor. Sidi's acceptance of the first effort and direction to carry lessons into later tours do not authorise a wholesale Finchley rewrite. The older review-close requirement has been changed at his request, but no trace proves it caused the reported manual chapter start. A full M2 closure would need an explicit combined-evidence decision on these remaining limits; this document does not make that decision.

**Consciously deferrable for this personal prototype:** exhaustive route-point verification, every handset/headset, battery endurance without an observed concern, telephony-specific routing, background voice recording, rare interrupted-capture recovery, physical fault injection for every importer path, automatic old-version cleanup and a generic cue-preemption engine. Preserve manual Play/Pause/End, skip/map/transcript and phone/text feedback fallbacks. Revisit a deferred item only when a real failure or the next chosen route makes it material. This does not defer a known unsafe instruction, private-data exposure, lost saved data or routine inability to complete.
