# Queensway — Behind the façades

**20–25 minutes · about 1.2 km · George · three stops and one short walking story.**

Start at **QUEENS, 17 Queensway**, about a minute north of Queensway station. Finish near **46 Queensborough Terrace**. The route takes in QUEENS, Whiteleys and Leinster Gardens, using public streets and passageways. No tickets or park opening hours are needed. Crossing waits or a leisurely pace can add a few minutes.

**Ready on your phone:** installed, selected and unstarted. The map and first directions passed the silent offline check; Wi-Fi and mobile data are restored.

## Start the demo

1. Choose **Queensway — Behind the façades** in the app.
2. Stand beside the QUEENS entrance, clear of the doorway and bus-stop space. Press **Start** there; the welcome plays immediately.
3. Keep automatic narration on and walk on after each story. Use **Read / directions** for the next leg and **Map and numbered stops** for the saved route.

The map, recordings, directions and source notes work offline once prepared. A deliberate Pause stays held until Resume. **I'm here / play story** is the fallback if a stop does not start. If the walking story is missed, play it while stopped; there is no need to retrace your steps.

## The route in brief

- Follow Queensway north from QUEENS. At Porchester Gardens, use the pedestrian signals across Queensway, then across Porchester Gardens. The Whiteleys stop looks back across Queensway at the long frontage.
- Continue via **Porchester Gardens → Porchester Terrace → Leinster Place → Leinster Gardens**. The full directions specify the pavement and crossings. The walking story starts after crossing into Leinster Gardens and turning right along its east pavement. Finish listening before Queen’s Gardens, the next side road on your left.
- The last stop is on the east pavement of **Leinster Gardens**, just before Craven Hill Gardens. The reader gives the precise place to look.

Pause whenever a crossing, entrance or traffic needs your attention. The app does not dynamically silence a manually played clip at a junction. Street layout and viewpoints were checked using maps and dated imagery; current works and evening visibility have not been observed on foot. Follow physical signs if the arrangement has changed.

## Back towards dinner

After the final story, tap **End tour / stop location**. The saved route continues about five minutes via **Craven Hill Gardens, Porchester Terrace and Queensborough Passage**. Keep the passage silent and watch for access vehicles in its eastern section. At Queensborough Terrace, turn left; number **46** is about **100 metres** along on the same pavement. Stay on the public pavement rather than entering a hotel.

If the passage is unexpectedly closed, use public pavements via Porchester Gardens to Queensborough Terrace, allowing a few extra minutes. The offline map covers that alternative; it does not calculate a new route.
