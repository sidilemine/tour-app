**Engineer proposal:** use the responsibilities below for the next actual authoring assignment. These are roles with concrete outputs, not necessarily five agents running simultaneously or a new orchestration platform. Keep parallel work independent; later stages consume reviewed inputs. The lead owns the whole experience and resolves conflicts.

| Responsibility | Input and bounded output | Lesson it addresses |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Survey and research | Begin with the public-tour survey. Produce candidate places/stories, claim-level sources, historical actors, dates, causal relationships and explicitly unresolved questions. Follow promising relationships beyond the first convenient source. | A documented lease restriction does not explain who developed a site, who leased it to whom, or who manages it today. |
| Curation and narrative planning | Select from the research; define why each place earns a visit, the connecting thread, chronological bearings, stop/leg rhythm and allowances for broader reflection. Give the writer an outline and rejected alternatives. | Individually interesting facts need an intelligible progression; a late thematic payoff should have earlier preparation. |
| Route and visitor-experience review | Work with the curator on stop order and practical standing areas. Inspect maps from the incoming approach; use dated street imagery for ambiguous junctions/views. Produce the pedestrian path, visible anchors, directions, timings and honest limits. | A map street name or good destination viewpoint does not ensure recognisable spoken directions. Parallel roads and awkward city junctions are ordinary work. |
| Narration writing | Use the evidence, outline and reviewed physical context. Write natural prose, useful dates, supported imagined scenes and transitions. Stay within the agreed time budgets and flag missing research instead of improvising it. | Evocation should reveal a different place/time; familiar activity does not need prolonged theatrical description. |
| Independent factual and experience review | Check source support, unexplained actors/relationships, orientation, unsupported access, continuity, measured timing and the value of being there. Return specific issues to the responsible role. | Self-review in the same pass can miss a weak explanation, generic digression or misleading instruction. |

## Handoffs and integration

1. Research and route discovery can overlap once there are concrete candidates; neither finalises the other's conclusions by assumption.
2. The curator and route reviewer agree a feasible outline before prose is polished. A better story can justify changing the route, and a poor physical experience can justify dropping a story.
3. The writer receives evidence and physical context, with questions clearly visible. Missing causal links go back to research; they are not filled with vague communal language.
4. Independent review covers the complete listening-and-walking sequence as well as individual claims. The lead resolves findings and checks the resulting tour as a whole so specialist outputs do not become disconnected fragments.
5. Generate audio after the outline, claims and directions are reviewed. Check actual narration duration, pronunciation and navigation margins; feed necessary changes back to the right role.
6. Use ordinary owner feedback to refine the process. Do not turn every detailed comment into repeated local polishing or a new physical-test campaign.

## Second-tour handoff refinements

The [18 September direct clarification](reviews/2026-09-18-second-tour-clarifications.md) adds concrete material for the proposed roles, without making a Finchley rewrite task:

- Research includes the current state of affairs, living businesses/institutions, named people's identities and documented motives. Track unanswered questions about decline or change rather than replacing them with a cheerful historical ending.
- The writer uses those identities and period differences to inform imagined colour, and connects local motives to the story's insight/theme. Context should shape the scene rather than become a disconnected biographical aside.
- Independent review asks whether a listener understands introduced names, whether the story returns to today's place, and whether the final insight actually follows from local evidence. Check dated business/access facts separately from durable history.

Keep the owner's detailed wording and examples available to the eventual guideline synthesis; these handoff prompts do not replace them.

## Digression budget: direction versus proposal

Sidi welcomes place-grounded material and broader thought together, with time caps preventing the latter from taking over. A proposed initial allowance is roughly 25% of a stationary story's spoken duration, excluding navigation. This number is **not yet agreed** and is not a universal standard. Test the approach in the next authoring work; historical explanation tied to the actual place remains core material even when it describes something vanished. The central question is whether visiting the place adds clear value over listening at home.

## Scope

This is a documented workflow proposal for future assigned tour creation. It does not start new agents now, require a new Finchley edition, implement a knowledge database or automate the full authoring factory. Those implementation decisions remain governed by the roadmap and the next concrete assignment. Product defects reported during the walk can be diagnosed separately from editorial learning.
