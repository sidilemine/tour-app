## The outing

Allow **75–90 minutes at a leisurely pace**, including the Farringdon approach, walking, listening and looking. The route is about **2.74 km**. A brisk, no-lingering version will be shorter; no timed waits, purchases or interior visits are required. A return to Farringdon adds roughly **12–15 minutes**; Angel is roughly **15–18 minutes** from the finish.

Start at the **Charterhouse exterior**, finish at **Exmouth Market**. Between them: Smithfield, the carved gin-making panels on Britton Street, St John's Gate, Clerkenwell Green, Woodbridge Chapel and the Ingersoll frontage. The walk combines small working details, buildings with changing uses and life here today.

**For the fullest choice of optional extras, Wednesday–Friday late morning or lunchtime works well.** The daytime tour does not depend on seeing the overnight meat trade. Exmouth's street stalls are listed for weekdays; weekend cafés and shops have their own hours. The exterior route still works at the weekend. The Charterhouse's free museum/chapel is normally Tuesday–Saturday, 10:30–16:30; the St John museum's main galleries normally Wednesday–Saturday. These visits extend the outing and are optional. [Charterhouse](https://thecharterhouse.org/visit-us/) · [St John museum](https://museumstjohn.org.uk/planning-your-visit/) · [Exmouth market hours](https://www.islington.gov.uk/business/markets).

## Just the useful feedback

Enjoy the tour first. There is no requirement to record at every stop or run an outdoor test checklist. The useful questions are:

- What stayed with you, or changed how you saw a place?
- What dragged, confused you, or felt weakly connected to being there?
- Did the walking stories start naturally and finish at a sensible place? If either needed manual Play, mention which one.

## A few practical route details

- The first story works outside the Charterhouse; the central garden and visitor rooms may be closed.
- The Clerkenwell Road crossing is at the **pedestrian signals beside Old Sessions House**, following the mapped approach from the Gate. Do not substitute a direct road crossing merely because the Green is close.
- On the Close, follow the bend right past the Three Kings, then turn right into Sans Walk. If works obstruct that section, use the saved **Sekforde Street fallback** in the Green's directions, skip the walking chapter, and play the chapel story manually if needed.
- The Ingersoll view uses the opposite pavement reached by the signals. For Exmouth, follow Skinner's north pavement, **cross Rosoman at the zebra**, turn right a few steps and then left into Exmouth.
- Current signs and clear pavement take precedence. The script does not depend on all five carved panels being unobscured or entering any building. This is a first personal walk of a desk-reviewed route; ordinary observations can settle current furniture, works and noise without a separate reconnaissance trip.
