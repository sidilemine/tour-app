## Interpretation boundaries

- The first review stored as Trinity is confirmed by the owner to concern Torrington. Preserve the saved record and original label; use the confirmed subject when interpreting its feedback. This does not independently establish the path walked.
- The Spotify observations concern different selected microphones. They are no longer an unexplained contradiction: the owner reports music continuing and poor saved audio with the headset microphone, then music stopping with the phone microphone. Native route/focus telemetry was not saved, so the precise mechanism remains unresolved.
- “Hipsters”, jazz and rockabilly are examples of questions research could answer, not descriptions to insert without support. The shop identities, dates, causes of decline and any new preservation campaign also need research before factual narration.
- Present-day relevance is a requirement in future authoring. It can honestly include decline and unanswered questions; it is not limited to cheerful codas or forced praise.
- The artsdepot point welcomes broader insight grounded in documented local motives. It does not ask for another generic digression or a compulsory moral at every stop.
