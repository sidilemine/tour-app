Both begin at Tally Ho Corner, a short walk north of North Finchley bus station, and finish outside artsdepot beside the station area. B is the more varied residential walk; A returns along more of the High Road. Neither needs tickets or indoor access. A's Arcade story works at the street entrance, so you do not need the passage to be open. Choose daylight for this first review so the buildings and sculpture are useful subjects.

## On the phone

1. Open **Walking Tour Lab**. If needed, choose **Prepare both Finchley tours offline** and wait for completion. Choose A or B. The engineer checks preparation before handoff.
2. Read the short introduction and open **Map and numbered stops**. Walk to Tally Ho Corner, then choose **Start tour at Tally Ho**. The audio, transcripts, directions and area map are local; airplane mode is fine with Location left on.
3. Listen at each exterior stop, then follow the spoken street-name directions. The map line is approximate and can follow road centres: use pavements and pedestrian crossings. **Read / directions** puts the approach, view and next directions before the transcript.
4. After a story, optionally choose **Review story**. Give whichever scores are useful and tap **Record voice note** only when ready. Around 20–40 seconds is plenty. Stop/save it; you can play the saved note to check it. Close the review and deliberately **Resume**. Saving never resumes narration. Locking or leaving the recording screen stops and saves the note.
5. On B, the area-history chapter begins after you leave the Meeting House along Alexandra Grove. It has its own review. If it is missed while paused or you walk beyond its useful window, **Play walking chapter** remains available; no need to walk backwards to test it.
6. If GPS misses a stop, choose **I’m here / play story**. Starting at a later stop explicitly skips earlier unfinished stops; they remain available to replay. Use **Skip** if a view is blocked. End at artsdepot and tap **End tour / stop location**.

If the app closes, reopen, start tracking and Resume deliberately. Each tour keeps separate progress; **Take this tour again** resets progress but keeps feedback. Do not uninstall or clear app storage to reset a walk.

## Useful feedback

This is ordinary use, not a compulsory checklist. Rate interest, value of being there and storytelling when convenient; zero differs from leaving a score blank. A quick voice note about what worked or what you would change is often more useful than a precise score. Mark shared stories “heard before” or skip duplicate feedback. There is no need to take both walks the same day.

At the end, one useful overall reaction is: **was the walk worth the time, and what one change would improve it?** Mention a confusing direction, obscured view, wrong/missed arrival or recording problem if encountered. A recoverable problem does not require repeating the whole walk.

All feedback stays on the phone unless you explicitly save/export a copy. Ratings/text export uses the named JSON save flow. Use **Save voice copy to folder** on any saved note to create a verified M4A copy. Its filename includes the note ID so it can be matched to the ratings JSON. Preserve the private originals until the feedback has been collected. No automatic upload or transcription occurs.
