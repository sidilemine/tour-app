## Material finding

### R01 — P02 alternative opening: restore the missing introduction

- **Type and location:** missing explanatory link; the alternative replaces paragraph 1, then joins the unchanged paragraph 2.
- **What the listener receives:** the alternative introduces a railway journey, home and Alexandra Grove. The next words are “From 1867, its estate…”, although no house or estate has been named. Moss Hall only appears later in that paragraph. The listener has to repair the connection while hearing new dates and the lease explanation.
- **Evidence:** P02-C01/C03/C06, independently checked in the [VCH history](https://www.british-history.ac.uk/vch/middx/vol6/pp38-55) and [2015 appraisal](https://open.barnet.gov.uk/download/2nx73/e21/2015-08-26%20-%20MossHallCrescentConservationArea%20-%20CharacterAppraisalAndManagementProposals.pdf), support the estate around the country house. The factual material is available; the replacement paragraph has removed its introduction.
- **Consequence:** the alternative cannot yet be substituted as instructed without weakening comprehension on one hearing.
- **Smallest useful remedy:** include a short introduction of Moss Hall as the country house in the alternative, or begin the following paragraph with its name and identity instead of “its”. Keep the railway opening available to Sidi. Count the complete substituted version afterwards.

No surviving-doorway finding applies to the frozen draft. The earlier working text had tied the directory to one of the front doors around the walker; the writer's finalisation replaced it before this review was completed. The present paragraph stays in a historical frame. Do not report that earlier concern as a defect of V1 or a change first made in response to this completed review.

## What should survive editing

**P01, paragraphs 4–5:** Duthie's planned combination of visiting productions, local groups and a meeting place leads naturally into Creative Circle and participant-made work. That is the strongest passage because it supplies a concrete answer to what more arts provision could offer. Paragraph 6 then turns the practical example into an inviting possibility. Preserve the distinction between the 1996 request, the director's 2004 offer and present provision; the current script manages it without sounding like research notes.

**P02, paragraph 3:** the directory's school entry earns the imagined books, chairs and beginning of conversation. The scene makes the residential street contain an activity, rather than merely another date. Preserve it, together with paragraph 2's concise surprise that the suburb arrived before the country house disappeared. The opening supplies a broad visible connection without making the listener hunt for an unverified historical door.
