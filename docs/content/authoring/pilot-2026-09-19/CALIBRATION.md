| --- | --- |

| Crescent treatment A | Too many short factual sentences with equal weight. A sentence or two can supply context; the whole story needs varied rhythm and connected thought. |

| Torrington's unnamed context and “the attraction here” | Explain who a person is; don't read the editor's rationale to the visitor. Look for a present-day connection. |

| Vanished scenes | Actively provide researched colour. A bare invitation to imagine does too little. Start with reconstruction and atmosphere; no invented dialogue or composite characters in this experiment. |
| Walking narration | A substantial part of the intended experience, with greater room for thought and one or two natural connections to surroundings. |
| Overall voice | Insightful documentary with warmth; 99% Invisible is a broad craft reference. Levity is welcome; avoid imposing a thesis or emotional reaction. |
