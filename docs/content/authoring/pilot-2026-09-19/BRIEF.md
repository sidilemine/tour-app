# A small tour authoring workshop

19 September 2026. Sidi approved trying the agent workflow on one stop story and one walking chapter, with the actual assignments, material, drafts and revisions visible. This is an M2 editorial process pilot. It does not select the next tour area or update an installed package.

## The commission

Prepare two independent Finchley excerpts using the existing [public-tour survey](../../survey/README.md), source and route records. The engineer chose familiar material to isolate the authoring work and make differences assessable. This is a workshop application of earlier feedback, not a renewed Finchley improvement backlog. They are not consecutive excerpts from a newly assembled tour.

- **P01 artsdepot stop:** explain what people sought from a local arts centre, connecting the vanished cinema, the campaign and the present place. Research may change this pitch. Aim initially for 280–360 words, roughly two to three minutes, with no need to pad. Visitor is outside near Nether Street; no interior access is assumed.
- **P02 residential walking chapter:** use the existing Meeting House to Moss Hall Crescent leg. Explore how a rural estate became streets and homes, with one or two broad visible connections and supported reconstruction. Route review sets the usable speech budget before writing. Avoid exhausting the destination's villa/garden story or claiming a collective development arrangement that evidence does not establish.

One walking sample here exercises the method; the next full-tour brief still calls for two narrated legs. Timing is a desk estimate until actual audio is generated. This assignment prepares prose and coordination records; no listening session or new physical test is necessary.

## Shared instructions

Read the current [writing brief](../WRITING-BRIEF.md) and relevant [role brief](../AGENT-BRIEFS.md). The [living guidance](../../TOUR-DESIGN-GUIDANCE.md) governs owner preferences. This task brief and current owner instructions supersede older proposal wording. Use the [calibration notes](CALIBRATION.md), not a guessed narrator personality.

Sidi is editorial lead. The coordinating agent assigns bounded tasks, checks handoffs and brings meaningful choices together. Researchers may deepen a promising lead and the writer may ask focused questions. Preserve the question and answer; do not manufacture a research loop merely to demonstrate collaboration. Keep original drafts and later revisions separate.

Use the existing claim/source IDs where possible and a local P01/P02 prefix for new pilot notes. Evidence belongs behind fluent narration. Reconstruction and atmosphere are the current imaginative scope. Return material gaps, conflicts and current-source limits honestly; do not turn a missing record into an unverified motive. Preserve useful lightness where the material allows it.

Each agent owns only its named pilot files. Do not edit package files, global guidance, another agent's outputs, original Word reviews or private feedback. Do not commit; the coordinator will review and commit the complete pilot. No paid services, public publication or private-data upload. Read-only public research is authorised.

## Completion for this assignment

The owner receives two readable sample scripts, meaningful editorial choices, the exact saved assignments and a clear account of what changed through research and review. Central factual claims have inspectable source support; route-specific statements retain their actual verification limits. An independent reviewer reads the material and sources, then the writer addresses material findings. Preserve discretionary disagreements for Sidi rather than polishing away all alternatives.

This pilot can show whether the handoffs functioned and caught particular problems. It cannot establish improved enjoyment, prove physical timing or compare agents experimentally with a single author. Those conclusions require appropriate later evidence.
