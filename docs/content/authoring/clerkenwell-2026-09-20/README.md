## What this teaches the process, provisionally

- A surveyed stop list is the beginning of research. The useful scenes came from object records, an actual notebook and institutional history; existing tours supplied leads rather than our narration.
- Curation has to happen within each place. Keeping all supported material would lose the story's main pleasure. The retained alternative should explain a choice, not become an appendix spoken at every stop.
- An exact evidence link cannot prevent the subject of a sentence drifting: the purchasing-attribution error survived structurally valid paragraph mappings. A fresh reader still matters.
- Route assembly and writing need a short feedback loop. Actual George timing and the latest possible launch govern the corridor, not estimated word counts or the mere existence of a long leg. Correcting one crossing can change the whole approach.
- Review can add a small present-day fact while subtracting repetitive explanation. It need not make prose more guarded or strip reasonable reconstruction.

These are coordinator observations from this execution, **not yet owner feedback about the finished walk or proven general rules**. Carry future reactions to the [editorial discussion record](../../EDITORIAL-REVIEW-RECORD.md) before promoting them into the living guidance. No database, autonomous factory or public release was introduced.

## Retained limits

Street imagery spans 2019–2025; official current pages were checked 20 September 2026. Exact furniture, temporary barriers, acoustic comfort and present sightlines await ordinary use. The Charterhouse/Smithfield pavement candidates rely more on mapped public edges and official access than satisfactory street-photo inspection; no private/interior panorama was used to claim public standing access. The fallback and manual controls make these ordinary first-use uncertainties proportionate, without pretending a map proves today's conditions.

All ten media files received exact paragraph-chunk, token, duration, full-decoder and hash checks. No reviewer claims to have heard every rendered word. Subjective delivery and local-name pronunciation remain part of the listening experience. Historical current-affairs wording, especially the museum's announced 28 November 2026 opening, must be refreshed before reusing the package after that change. A later source/content change needs a new package version after import.
