# Clerkenwell route and view research

Research date: 20 September 2026. Desk research for **Working lives, hidden in plain sight**; George narration; 75–90 minutes in the area, excluding travel. This is a route recommendation and evidence record, not a field-verified pavement plan or an installable package. Story research is in the neighbouring research files. No private location traces were used.

## Recommendation for the writer

Use **Charterhouse Square → Smithfield → Booth reliefs, Britton Street → St John's Gate → Clerkenwell Green → Woodbridge Chapel → Ingersoll frontage → Exmouth Market**. Include a written Farringdon approach. **Press Start at Charterhouse, not at the station:** current playback starts the first stop immediately. Finish beside the church/Exmouth Arms part of Exmouth; a station return is optional and outside the narrated route.

Between Green and Woodbridge, take Clerkenwell Close past St James's churchyard and the Three Kings, then Sans Walk and Woodbridge Street. This earns its modest extra distance through changing street scale, churchyard views and workshop surroundings. The square, Gate exterior, Green and Close offer opportunities to look; a paid or timed interior visit is not needed. Do not prescribe an extra loop to reach a duration.

The selected desk route is **2.739 km / 38.2 minutes of movement**, including the Farringdon approach. It combines `selected-south`, `gate-signals` and `selected-north`; the selected north request removes the false Sans Walk reversal and pins Close to its eastern/inner pavement and Skinner Street to its north pavement. Distances remain estimates, and visitor positions remain desk candidates with current furniture/works uncertainty. Budget **16–20 minutes of stationary audio plus 18–25 minutes of looking, crossing waits and settling**: approximately **72–83 minutes**, with 75–90 plausible at leisure. Walking audio overlaps movement and is **not added again**. Ninety minutes is not a minimum content duration; refreshments/interiors are extra.

The saved compact route is **1.828 km / 25.3 minutes**: Farringdon, Smithfield, Britton, Gate, Green, direct to Woodbridge, Exmouth. Correcting its Gate crossing adds approximately 57 m / 0.9 min.
