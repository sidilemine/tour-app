## The walk's shape

Begin at the Charterhouse exterior after a mapped approach from Farringdon. Follow different ways of earning, making and sustaining a life through Smithfield, Britton Street, St John's Gate, the Green, Woodbridge, Ingersoll and Exmouth. The opening residential community and present-day shops give the historical workplaces a living frame. Allow curiosity, skill and wit to carry the tour; it does not need one social moral at every stop.

Eight candidates now earn consideration independently of the target duration. The initial six made a shorter walk; the extra square and retained watch-trade sign add attractive places and different subjects. Current provider geometry for the longer sequence is roughly 2.6 km/35.5 minutes at its assumed pace, before approach corrections. A relaxed walk plus selective stories and looking can approach 75–90 minutes. Ninety minutes is not a guaranteed content floor; do not count optional interiors or feedback recording as required tour time.

## Two walking passages

**1. Green → Woodbridge: many hands, small parts.** Use the researched division of watch work to explain a district of specialists and exchanges, rather than another factory. Root the opening in the surrounding streets without claiming a particular visible window was a workshop. Allow roughly 75–100 seconds initially; route review and measured George delivery must determine the final safe launch/end budget. The nearby Ingersoll stop then concerns a concrete object and distribution, not a repeat of the same craft explanation.

**2. Ingersoll → Exmouth: the next day's stock.** Reset clearly to fresh-flower street sellers, returning to the earlier local mission evidence from a different angle. Their purchasing, loans and perishable stock make commercial judgement tangible; the approach to a street of current small businesses gives the thought somewhere to land. Roughly 75–100 seconds initially, pending the actual corridor. Avoid equating contemporary traders' conditions with the Victorian scheme.

Minor quiet turns can be explained before departure; not every corner demands a generic interruption engine. Complex crossings and ambiguous decisions need attention and a clear audio-free margin. The current player can finish a chapter after its launch interval, so its *latest possible launch* must still leave enough room before the relevant navigation decision. No route detour is justified merely to create narration time.
