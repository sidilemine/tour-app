## Useful process observations

The route review corrected a published discovery pin about 55 metres north of the actual numbered façade. It also found small junctions breaking an apparently quiet walking corridor, so the chapter moved to a checked straight section of Leinster Gardens. Candidate coordinates and apparent road quietness do not establish a usable visitor point or uninterrupted listening window. See the general [issues log](../../../ISSUES.md) for reusable safeguards.

Preserve the final reveal in neutral stop naming. The archival party picture is described within what the museum actually documents; no invented party date, skating in swimsuits or placement on the ice. Narration never promises a free interior visit or relies on gardens being open after dinner.
