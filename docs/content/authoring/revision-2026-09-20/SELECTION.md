**Connected outline:** locate the present building → enter the vanished cinema through its scale and rising organ → let the demolition create an actual break → establish the request, then the theatre choice and campaign → show what opened → follow writing from group to stage. **Gains and losses:** retain the pilot's clearer explanation, brief 1996 origin and current writing group; replace its ordinary light-dimming with a live performer rising into view; restore the campaign. The first selection omitted the consultation, but a whole-story read left the proposed theatre arriving without an origin; one sentence restores that useful join. Omit opening stars' names and titles so the cinema's programme remains understandable without becoming another cast list. Funding disputes would need a separate developed account. No “free” gig, exclusive protest causation or all-arts-versus-no-arts claim.
