Date: 21 September 2026. Researcher: `hampstead_route`. **Selected route, desk-reviewed; access has not been field-observed.** Owner changed the start from Hampstead to **Highgate Underground station**. No phone data or private trace was used.

[Selection](SELECTION.md) chooses five stops: Highgate station, Pond Square, the Highgate pond causeway, 2 Willow Road exterior, and Keats House street frontage. The mapped finish is Hampstead Heath station. The Grove, Kenwood, Burgh House, a sixth pond story, and museum/garden entry are omitted.

- **Visitor directions:** [ROUTE-DIRECTIONS.md](ROUTE-DIRECTIONS.md). Raw provider maneuvers are audit evidence, not direct user instructions.
- **Distance/time:** original provider estimates total **4,078 m / 58.31 minutes** to Hampstead Heath station, including **3,836 m / 55.03 minutes** to Keats House. The joined, deduplicated and locally corrected route geometry measures **4,043 m**. These are different measurement products, not a claim of metre-level surveyed accuracy. Allow about **85–100 minutes** with stories, looking and crossings; no numeric ascent is claimed.
- **Optional Northern line finish:** Keats to Hampstead Underground is **822 m / 13.53 router minutes**, versus **242 m / 3.28 minutes** to Hampstead Heath station: approximately **580 m / 10¼ minutes extra**.
- **First chapter:** Merton Lane imagery changed the plan. Its narrow carriageway has no continuous footway, so Merton and Millfield remain silent. Narration launches after the actual right turn onto the shared Heath path. The pond pause moved 18 m west to provide **128.94 m / 77.36 seconds at 6 km/h** after the latest launch. A **60-second cap** preserves **17.36 seconds** before the stop.
- **Second chapter:** launches after the initial path intersection, on the main cross-Heath path. The latest launch leaves **269.61 m / 161.76 seconds at 6 km/h** before the required left turn; a **90-second cap** leaves **71.76 seconds**. Both windows retain the 15-second minimum margin, without walking narration on a road crossing.

The first provider line crossed East Heath Road directly opposite Downshire Hill. It was replaced with the mapped approach to the **zebra just west of that junction**, observed in September 2025 imagery. OSM lacks its south-side pavement connector: the selected line explicitly adds the mapped south landing and a short approximate connector supported by the photographed pavement layout. `authoredGeometryCorrections` records those two changes; raw responses and hashes remain unchanged. Street-centreline routing elsewhere does not override pavement/crossing directions.

All five visitor points are public standing **candidates** checked against the available maps/imagery. The pond pause lacks an inspected panorama, so no exact clear width or island sightline is promised. The first chapter and pond story work without a direct island look cue. Willow's building-side pavement is visible; its optional opposite bench is not required. ## Earlier comparison (superseded by selected result)

A first comparison puts both lean one-way options at roughly **4.2–4.4 km from Highgate station**, before small waypoint corrections. At 4.5 km/h the router estimates **61–63 minutes of movement**; allow roughly **90–105 minutes overall** for useful stationary stories and looking. Hills, uneven surfaces and crossing waits can lengthen this. The earlier 75–90-minute Clerkenwell preference is not a mandate to mislabel this route.

- **A:** station → Highgate village/Pond Square → The Grove → Highgate West Hill → Merton Lane → Heath ponds → east–west Heath path → Well Walk → Burgh exterior → Hampstead Underground. Preliminary village portion 3.205 km / 47.1 minutes, plus station approach 0.944 km / 14.2 minutes.
- **B:** same eastern start → Heath crossing → Hampstead ponds → 2 Willow Road exterior → Keats exterior → Hampstead Heath Overground. Preliminary village portion 3.437 km / 48.7 minutes, plus the same station approach.

**These first provider lines were rejected.** A Grove point snapped to an unwanted northern alley; Well Walk snapped to the fountain's stepped passage; the first Hampstead endpoint snapped underground; the B Keats leg took an avoidable South End Road route. The selected route pins public approaches and avoids those snaps. It uses the main made/shared east–west path between the Model Boating and Men's ponds, shown on the City's official map, then the southwest connection towards the Hampstead ponds.

## Access findings that already change decisions

- Highgate station is a separate start from Highgate village. The Archway Road **exit 3** is mapped at 51.577603, -0.146800. September 2025 imagery confirms the street frontage and public pavement; use a clear space beside the exit rather than the railway pin. Archway Road has pedestrian signals by Southwood Lane. No instruction to cross the A1 arbitrarily.
- Use **Merton Lane** for the efficient Highgate-to-Heath entry, not the much farther south mouth of Millfield Lane. The [North London Hospice 2025 event route](https://northlondonhospice.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/BFW_MAP2025_v4.pdf) explicitly descends Highgate West Hill, crosses by a refuge and enters the Heath at Merton Lane's end. This is route-use evidence, not today's clearance.
- [Highgate Society's November 2023 pavement audit](https://highgatesociety.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/Traffic-walk-Chetwynd-to-South-Grove.pdf) describes very narrow west-side pavement above Merton Lane. Keep this descent silent; prefer the eastern pavement until the refuge south of Merton's mouth. **September 2025 Street View actually inspected** confirms Merton's mouth, the refuge just south, and the change from pavement to rough verge on its north corner. The refuge is uncontrolled: it is not a zebra or signalised crossing and does not give pedestrian priority.
- The [City's official Heath map](https://www.cityoflondon.gov.uk/assets/Green-Spaces/hampstead-heath-map.pdf), visually inspected as a rendered PDF, distinguishes made/unmade/shared paths. It shows the principal east–west shared route between Model Boating and Men's ponds, connecting towards Saxon Ditch/Hampstead ponds. Do not treat every OSM thin path as an equally dependable walking corridor.
- Heath entry does not require entry into a bathing enclosure. The [official Men's Pond page](https://newsite.cityoflondon.gov.uk/things-to-do/green-spaces/hampstead-heath/where-to-go-at-hampstead-heath/highgate-mens-pond) describes unmade access tracks from roads. Do not promise a flat, paved or wheelchair-accessible tour. Mixed surfaces, slopes, roots/mud and cyclists on shared paths remain ordinary-use considerations.
- Monday is unsuitable for a tour depending on interiors. [Fenton House](https://www.nationaltrust.org.uk/visit/london/fenton-house-and-garden) generally opens Friday/Sunday/bank-holiday Monday; [2 Willow Road](https://www.nationaltrust.org.uk/visit/london/2-willow-road/booking-your-visit-to-2-willow-road) Thursday/Saturday by booking. [Burgh's current 2026 exhibition page](https://www.burghhouse.org.uk/whats-on/exhibitions/hampstead-artists-residents-and-history) lists Wednesday/Thursday/Friday/Sunday. [Keats official 2025 announcement](https://news.cityoflondon.gov.uk/centenary-exhibition-at-keats-house-invites-visitors-to-journey-back-to-1925/) lists Wednesday/Thursday/Friday/Sunday; its [current visit page](https://www.cityoflondon.gov.uk/things-to-do/attractions-museums-entertainment/keats-house/visit-keats-house) has useful access details but no opening schedule in retrieved text. Keep both candidate tours entirely exterior-based; a free garden is not necessarily open on Monday.

## Reproducible route data

Public candidate requests/responses and original OSM extracts are saved privately/ignored under `diagnostics/hampstead-route-2026-09-21/`. `highgate-A` and `highgate-B` identify the initial comparisons; `highgate-approach` measures the station-to-village link. `highgate-village` is an earlier overlong hybrid and must not be selected. Raw responses are preserved, including rejected snaps. All requests use the [Valhalla public service](https://valhalla1.openstreetmap.de/) with pedestrian costing, 4.5 km/h and en-GB output; API semantics checked against [official documentation](https://valhalla.github.io/valhalla/api/route/api-reference/). OpenStreetMap data © [OpenStreetMap contributors](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright), ODbL.

A rendered original analysis plot and the official PDF render are local research aids, not a distributable map asset or proof of safe access. Selected raw legs are `chosen-east`, `ready-merton`, `ready-heath`, `chosen-keats`, and `return-heath-final`; the written Underground alternative is `return-hampstead-ready`. The committable JSON retains query locations, response hashes and the explicit authored crossing correction. Earlier `final-*` / `chosen-merton` / `chosen-heath` filenames are intermediate research, not the selected result. Join distances, stop associations, launch windows and fast-walking margins were checked locally; no app or physical acceptance result is claimed by this documentation work.
