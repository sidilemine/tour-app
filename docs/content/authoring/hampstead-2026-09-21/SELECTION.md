## Experience and route

**Highgate to Hampstead — Room to breathe.** A loose portrait of people making room for enjoyment, imagination and a different daily life within London. The theme connects the material without requiring every ending to teach the same lesson.

Choose the southern crossing to **2 Willow Road and Keats House**, with a short mapped finish to Hampstead Heath station/South End Green. Hampstead Underground is an optional longer onward walk. The alternatives were Well Walk/Burgh and a Hampstead tube finish, or an expanded route including both clusters. Keats and Willow offer a stronger contrast of human stories; joining both endings adds walking and weakens the selective outing. Kenwood, Parliament Hill, Waterlow, upper Hampstead and Pergola remain worthwhile material for other walks.

Five stops, including a short station welcome, and two walking passages are the working selection. The substantial Heath crossing also has deliberate quiet. A western pond story is not added simply to increase the stop count. Current timing is approximately an hour of walking before stationary narration, looking and breaks; the final estimate will use corrected geometry and measured George audio. A provisional 75–90-minute outing is an aim, not an instruction to distort the route.
