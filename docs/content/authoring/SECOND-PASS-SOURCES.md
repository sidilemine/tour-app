# Research notes for the second craft pass

18 September 2026. Companion to [Making a walk worth taking](../TOUR-STORY-CRAFT-SECOND-PASS.md). This is a focused expansion into interest, radio, documentary and location-based art, not a systematic review. References match the discussion paper. The earlier [source map](SOURCE-MAP.md) preserves all 45 captured Word comments; no owner input is displaced by an outside convention.

## Search and reading scope

Searches covered first-party radio/storytelling guidance, documentary editing and place, artists' location-based work, and original interest research. The new review favours practitioner accounts and primary creative material over generic writing-advice summaries. Their value is craft insight, not proof that our tours will improve. Film craft is studied through the linked interviews and articles; films and embedded audio were not newly watched/listened to.

The request was dictated with several unclear phrases. The working interpretation is a deeper second pass to support two more hand-built **tours**, including wider writing/film lessons. An early clarification question and an optional familiar/unfamiliar-area question were sent; preparation can proceed without selecting a destination. This interpretation is not recorded as an answer from Sidi.

## Source register

### 1 · Silvia: interest and comprehensibility

Paul J. Silvia, 2005, *Emotion* 5(1), 89–102, DOI 10.1037/1528-3542.5.1.89. [Author's research abstract indexed by PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15755222/).

**Read:** indexed abstract. University reprint retrieval failed; no full-methods review claimed. **Supporting phrase:** “the ability to understand the new, complex thing”. **Use:** distinguish unfamiliarity from incomprehensibility, and interest from enjoyment. **Limit:** experimental stimuli differ from a walking tour; no optimal difficulty, pacing or narration length follows.

### 2 · The Moth: consequences in personal storytelling

[Storytelling Tips & Tricks](https://www.themoth.org/tell-your-story/storytelling-tips-tricks), undated, accessed 18 September 2026.

**Read:** complete short guidance. **Supporting phrase:** “What do you stand to gain or lose?” **Use:** ask what matters to the people in an episode. **Limit:** its first-person, timed, true-story performance format is specific; do not import its exclusions of essays or other forms into all tour narration.

### 3 · Ross: an experience of place

Max Fraser, [Filming the Black Belt: An Interview with RaMell Ross](https://dissentmagazine.org/article/filming-the-black-belt-an-interview-with-ramell-ross/), *Dissent*, Fall 2019.

**Read:** interview, especially initial purpose, long observation and editing. **Supporting phrase:** “experience of a place”. **Use:** counterweight to compulsory dramatic plots. **Limit:** a situated filmmaking practice, not a recipe for quickly representing another community or proof of tour enjoyment.

### 4 · Glass: material and meaning

Ira Glass, [Transom manifesto](https://transom.org/2004/ira-glass/), 1 June 2004.

**Read:** manifesto sections on story construction, finding ideas, weak pitches and exceptions; not the later discussion thread or embedded recordings. **Supporting phrase:** “understanding what it is that attracts you to the story”. **Use:** discover the compelling material and connect events with reflection. **Limit:** advice for his radio practice, explicitly allowing other approaches; not a universal conflict rule.

### 5 · Darbyshire / VoiceMap: a craft repertoire to adapt

[Writing scripts that make listeners feel something](https://docs.voicemap.me/tour-publishers/writing-scripts-that-make-listeners-feel-something/), attributed to Tom Darbyshire's March 2026 webinar.

**Read:** written article; no webinar/listening claim. **Supporting phrase:** “use it deliberately, not by default”. **Use:** consider scenes, active language and chaptering. **Limit:** commercial producer advice; its broad psychology claims are not independently established here. Prescriptions about openings, numbers of names and sound remain options rather than our rules.

### 6 · VoiceMap: revisable route and script

[Deciding on a route](https://docs.voicemap.me/tour-publishers/deciding-on-a-route/), undated, accessed 18 September 2026.

**Read:** introduction and walking guidance. **Supporting phrase:** “more pencil than permanent marker”. **Use:** develop physical route and story together. **Limit:** its repeated route walks, duration advice and speech density do not supersede Sidi's testing/time preferences.

### 7 · Cardiff / Miller: location as part of the medium

[Walks](https://cardiffmiller.com/walks/), artists' own overview, accessed 18 September 2026.

**Read:** explanatory overview, not every individual artwork. **Supporting phrase:** “the same route they are traversing in the present”. **Use:** consider the relationship between recording and current surroundings. **Limit:** deliberate fiction and perceptual confusion have a different contract from sourced heritage and literal navigation; no new soundscape feature is proposed.

### 8 · 99% Invisible: inspect an actual example

[Curb Cuts transcript](https://99percentinvisible.org/episode/308-curb-cuts/transcript/), page dated 27 April 2021.

**Read:** programme opening, the development of the street feature's significance, and later wider applications. **Supporting phrase:** “you probably don't pay much attention”. **Use:** examine movement from an overlooked object to people and a larger explanation. **Limit:** craft analysis of a published transcript, not independent historical verification or a new audio assessment.

### 9 · BFI / documentary editors: selection and rhythm

Nikki Baughan, [In praise of the undersung art of documentary editing](https://www.bfi.org.uk/features/praise-undersung-art-documentary-editing), 20 December 2019.

**Read:** opening and relevant film/editing sections, especially first-person comments about *The Cave*. **Supporting phrase:** “a specific talk about contents and editing style”. **Use:** discuss actual selected scenes early and preserve variation in intensity. **Limit:** the article includes criticism as well as practitioners' testimony; neither demonstrates outcomes for tours.

### 10 · VoiceMap: spoken editing

[Writing for voice](https://docs.voicemap.me/tour-publishers/writing-for-voice/), undated, accessed 18 September 2026.

**Read:** guidance and written examples; embedded audio not assessed. **Supporting phrase:** “Read your script aloud often”. **Use:** make the listening version the object of review. **Limit:** illustrative practice; the examples are not historical source material or an argument for shallow prose.

### 11 · Barrett: researching the people named in an advert

[Official press biography](https://www.wildwillybarrett.com/press), accessed 18 September 2026.

**Read:** retrospective biography and current promotional descriptions. **Supporting phrase:** “developed a following on the pub rock circuit”. **Use:** attributed context for the duo's performance identity in the Torrington research demonstration. **Limit:** self-presentation, not independent contemporary reporting. Present-day stage props and an anecdote about a television performance are not evidence of the advertised local concert. No exact television chronology is adopted.

### 12 · Official Charts: a dated comparison

[Really Free chart history](https://www.officialcharts.com/songs/john-otway-and-wild-willy-barrett-really-free/), accessed 18 September 2026.

**Read:** chart facts and run. **Supporting data:** first chart date 3 December 1977; peak position 27. **Use:** the June advert precedes this chart success. **Limit:** no claim follows about attendance, local audience knowledge, or the concert actually happening. The source of the advert remains the existing story evidence record; it was not newly authenticated here.

## Leads considered but not used as substantive evidence

- George Loewenstein, *The Psychology of Curiosity* (1994): author-hosted PDF retrieval failed. Search snippets were insufficient for a fresh methodological review. The paper is not used to certify a curiosity mechanism in our proposed method.
- Ursula K. Le Guin, [The Carrier Bag Theory of Fiction](https://www.ursulakleguin.com/the-carrier-bag-theory-of-fiction): official description read, not the full essay. Overlapped with the stronger directly applicable sources above; not added merely to enlarge the bibliography.
- John Otway's official biography/discography appeared in indexed search results, but direct biography retrieval failed. A contemporary NME review scan was also inaccessible. Neither is used to establish a local performance. The probe uses the directly readable Barrett biography and chart record; it is sufficient to illustrate a changed pitch, not a completed music-history investigation.

## How owner evidence shaped the analysis

- Survey first remains the starting method; subsequent discovery can seek overlooked life and alternative explanations.
- Tally Ho prompts context and historical hierarchy, without mechanically giving every story an encyclopaedic opening.
- Crescent demonstrates both successful enjoyment and a missing account of the arrangement. The three new sketches retain those factual limits; their appeal is untested.
- Torrington prompts research that changes the scene, not decorative biography or assumed audience detail.
- Trinity prompts specific institutional relationships and sensory consistency; tea-and-cake is not a reusable formula.
- Arcade prompts present-day reporting and causal restraint, including decline rather than a positive-only ending.
- artsdepot prompts the link from documented motivation to grounded insight, without inventing that missing motivation.
- Walking-chapter enthusiasm, variable spacing and quiet remain positive design opportunities. Short technical tests do not define product length.
