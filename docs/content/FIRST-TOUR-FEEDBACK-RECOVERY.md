## Outcome and limits

Useful, coherent feedback was recovered for **all six stops and the walking chapter**. A complete re-recording is unnecessary. Some words and several detailed passages remain uncertain; the three specific clarification questions below should take priority over repeating whole memos.

The private app archive contains **14 field recordings totalling 19 minutes 29 seconds**, plus the separate seven-second desk recording from the earlier phone check. All 14 field files decode fully; their extracted bytes match the preserved archive. Ratings are intact for all seven subjects. Very short recording attempts are included in that count, not treated as additional substantive reviews.

Recovery used local [whisper.cpp](https://github.com/ggml-org/whisper.cpp) 1.9.1: large-v3-turbo Q8 on original audio, followed by medium.en Q5 on filtered copies of the 11 files longer than ten seconds. Filtering reduced low-frequency noise and applied mild noise reduction and level adjustment. No private audio was uploaded or sent to a subscription service. Originals were not altered. Timestamps, raw machine transcripts, model/file hashes and copies remain in ignored `diagnostics/finchley-first-walk-2026-09-17/`.

**These are reviewed paraphrases of machine transcripts, not a certified verbatim transcription or owner-approved guidelines.** Agreement between two recognisers helps identify usable themes but cannot prove every word. Misheard names, garbled phrases and uncertain historical suggestions have not been silently converted into facts. Audio after a recording stopped cannot be recovered from that file.

## Recovered editorial feedback

### 1. Tally Ho

- Good subject and worthwhile being at the place, but the telling felt disjointed. Strengthen the logical and chronological connections: the junction, its coaching role, the cycling club/anecdote, and the name attached to the corner.
- Explain why one development follows another rather than announcing a loosely connected “coaching connection”. The cycling group's place in a changing London is potentially more interesting than treating the story as two isolated cyclists.
- The voice mispronounces Marylebone; Sidi treated this as a minor issue for later.
- Directions need an understandable starting orientation and visible reference, rather than street names alone. The proposed Tally Ho Hotel/facing-direction example is partially garbled and is not a verified replacement instruction.
- Any suggested historical linkage or period in the memo remains a research lead. Do not treat transcription of a suggested explanation as evidence for that explanation.

### 2. Trinity

- Anchor this and other stops with where the listener is and what they are looking at. A little context about the church/building would help before the rehearsal story.
- Too much elaboration of an ordinary rehearsal felt forced. The permission to use imagined colour particularly concerned vanished/historical scenes that are harder to picture; familiar activities need less imaginative explanation.
- The sourced tea-and-cake detail was welcome as an inside view.
- Research the actual relationship between the church and the orchestra/music: an established association, a particular musical role, or merely an event happening there. If there is no deeper connection, accept that rather than inventing one.

### 3. Meeting House sculpture

- Strong positive response to the artwork, detail, narration and the value of seeing it in place.
- Reflect on the proportion devoted to the actual place/artwork versus broader ideas such as art-making or environmentalism. The precise proposed rule is unclear; do not turn this into a fixed quota or assume Sidi disliked the balance of this particular story.
- Give a little context about Alistair Lambert: who he is, any local connection and why he was chosen, if research supports it.

- A possible relationship between Quaker values and the artwork's environmental theme was raised, but the connecting passage is noisy. Research and confirm it rather than asserting a religious interpretation.

### 4. Walking chapter

## Reported app issues — separate from editorial feedback

- Sidi expected the headphones' microphone to record. The app currently does not explicitly select or display a recording input; pinned native source defaults to `MediaRecorder.AudioSource.MIC`. This makes a phone-microphone explanation plausible, but the saved files/metadata do **not** prove which microphone was routed during the walk.
- The memos report capture stopping on phone locking and on opening the camera. Current code deliberately saves/stops when the app backgrounds. That matches the reported class of behaviour; it does not prove every short recording had that cause. This design is inconvenient for these longer comments.
- The Tally Ho memo requests that recording stop Spotify playback. A later Trinity memo wonders whether pausing Spotify itself stopped recording. These are two distinct reports, not an established single audio-focus diagnosis.
- The walking chapter reportedly required a manual start, with a less clear reference to other automatic starts not working. Saved final state has automatic narration enabled, six stops and the chapter completed, tracking off and a review hold. Field diagnostic logging was off, so old retained logs cannot establish the walk's arrival/hold sequence. Completion is not proof of automatic arrival success. Review intentionally holds narration until explicit Resume; whether that explains this outing needs a targeted follow-up, not an assumed diagnosis.
- No app code, narration or route was changed during this recovery task. Future fixes should address the observed recording/navigation needs with the smallest useful check, not require another full baseline walk.
