# Take the Highgate-to-Hampstead tour

**Room to breathe** · George · five stops and two short walking stories.

**Prepared, 21 September:** the complete tour and offline map are built. The silent phone installation/check is pending; see the [handoff record](../test-results/M2-hampstead-tour.md).

## The outing

Allow **85–100 minutes**, around **4.1 km**. Start at **Highgate Underground station, Archway Road exit 3**; finish by **Hampstead Heath station / South End Green**. The route goes through Highgate village and across the southern Heath to Willow Road and Keats House. Expect some slopes, a short narrow lane and Heath paths; several forks are unnamed, so keep the offline map available.

The stories combine an absurd old tavern ceremony, trees turned into London's plumbing, two poets on a walk, the work of keeping the Heath open, a controversial modern home and a way of sharing birdsong. This is a selective crossing, leaving time to look around; it does not visit Kenwood, Parliament Hill or Highgate Cemetery.

Everything works from public paths and pavements. No tickets, purchases or interior visits are required. **For today's Monday outing, treat 2 Willow Road and Keats House as exterior stops.** Keats's current exhibition can be visited another day; [official visiting information](https://www.cityoflondon.gov.uk/things-to-do/attractions-museums-entertainment/keats-house/visit-keats-house) gives the opening days. The green gate and part of the house can be seen from the pavement even when the garden is closed.

## Starting and listening

1. Choose **Highgate to Hampstead — Room to breathe** in the self-contained app. Your other tours and reviews stay available.
2. At Highgate station, use **Archway Road exit 3**. Stand clear of the entrance, then press **Start**. The welcome plays immediately, before crossing Archway Road at the nearby pedestrian signals.
3. Keep automatic narration on. Walk on after each story finishes; a deliberate Pause stays held until you press Resume.

**Read / directions** gives the approach, standing place and onward steps at each stop. The map and transcripts work offline. **I'm here / play story** is the fallback if a stop does not start. A missed walking passage can be played manually; there is no need to retrace your steps to trigger it.

The first walking story begins **after the turn from Millfield Lane onto the Heath path between the ponds**. The second is farther west after the pond stop. They last about **50 and 68 seconds**. Crossings and the narrow lane have no planned walking narration. Pause whenever you need your attention elsewhere; the app does not dynamically stop a manually played clip for a junction.

## The few directions worth noticing

- From the station, use the **Archway Road pedestrian signals**, Southwood Lane, then the **Highgate High Street zebra** before entering Pond Square.
- Leaving the square, descend Highgate West Hill on its **eastern pavement**. Continue just beyond Merton Lane to the **central refuge**, cross when each half is clear, then return a few steps uphill to the lane. The refuge is an uncontrolled crossing.
- **Merton Lane has no continuous footway.** Watch for vehicles. At its end turn left onto Millfield Lane, then right after about 200 metres onto the main Heath path between the ponds.
- Across the Heath, use the saved route and detailed steps at the unnamed forks. Stay on public paths; no swimming enclosure entry is needed.
- At East Heath Road, use the **zebra just west of Downshire Hill**, finish crossing, then turn left along the far pavement to Downshire Hill and Willow Road.

Current signs and available paths take precedence. The route has had a map and dated-imagery review; today's temporary works, exact sightlines and path conditions have not been observed on foot.

## Finishing and feedback

Keats House is the last narrated stop. Tap **End tour / stop location**. The offline route continues about **four minutes** along Keats Grove and right into South End Road to **Hampstead Heath station**. If you prefer the Northern line, the saved directions give an alternative **roughly 14-minute walk to Hampstead Underground**; that alternative is not the mapped finish.

Enjoy the outing first. No technical checklist or recording at every stop is needed. What helps afterward is what stayed with you, what dragged or confused you, whether the Heath forks were clear, and whether the walking stories started and finished naturally. If you needed manual Play, just mention where.

The EarFun microphone and Spotify interruption worked in the last short phone check. If recording sounds poor outdoors, use **Phone microphone** near your mouth or a written note. Keep Review visible while speaking: locking or leaving it stops and saves the recording. **Save review and resume tour** resumes an active tour. A short note and later conversation are enough.
