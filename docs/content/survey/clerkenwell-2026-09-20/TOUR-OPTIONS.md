# Two ways into Clerkenwell

20 September 2026. Sidi has selected **Clerkenwell / Farringdon**. These proposals follow the [public-walk survey](README.md); they are our editorial synthesis of the discoveries, not copies of an existing tour. Neither is a final itinerary. Historical mechanisms, individual stories, present views and physical directions still need the focused research and route work that follow selection.

**Owner decision, 20 September:** develop **A — Working lives, hidden in plain sight** first. The comparison and omissions below are retained as the basis for that choice. B is parked, not rejected as a future tour.

## My recommendation: start with A

**A gives us more varied opportunities for the kind of telling detail you have been asking for.** A working day, an object made by hand, a building adapted to a new purpose: each can supply something the listener would otherwise struggle to picture. B offers a stronger developing question and a useful alternative if that appeals more. Both should be enjoyable walks; neither needs to prove a predetermined moral.

| | A — **Working lives, hidden in plain sight** | B — **Making room for a better life** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Promise | Discover the work behind familiar-looking streets: feeding, making, printing, earning and spending. | Explore what people tried to provide for one another—and what those places offer now. |
| Shape | A portrait through contrasting activities and people, with connections emerging between them. | A developing question about how a crowded district makes room for health, livelihood, pleasure and culture. |
| Geographic emphasis | Smithfield and lower St John Street through the Green/Close towards Exmouth. Keep the western and far-northern extensions optional. | Green/Close, Woodbridge/Pine Street, Spa Fields/Exmouth and potentially New River Head. A northern finish could suit this shape. |
| Main opportunity | Vivid, materially different scenes; broader ideas arise from particular work. | A stronger cumulative ending, with the present-day health and illustration venues giving the history somewhere to land. |
| Main risk | Becoming a list of trades. Give each story its own attraction and make the joins do useful work. | Becoming a dutiful procession of worthy institutions. Preserve pleasure, awkward choices, humour and conflicting evidence. |

## A: candidate material worth developing

These are research candidates, not a six-item quota or committed order.

- **Smithfield:** investigate a working day organised around feeding the city, then distinguish current meat trading from the museum conversion. The two timelines prevent a falsely tidy “market becomes museum” ending. Source leads: `CW-PW-P-smithfield`, `CW-LC-P18/P30/P31`.
- **The gin reliefs / lower St John Street:** begin with a visible object and explain a process or trade through it. Strong potential for researched reconstruction; the reliefs' content and actual sightline need checking. `CW-PW-P-booths`.
- **St John's Gate:** choose one developed episode from its changing uses, likely print and publishing, rather than a roll call of illustrious visitors. Its many published treatments make curation especially important. `CW-PW-P-stjohn-gate`, `CW-LC-P17`.
- **The Green and its neighbouring rooms:** consider how words became public activity—print, meetings or organisation—while acknowledging the living square. Select one concrete event or mechanism after research. `CW-PW-P-green/marx`, `CW-LC-P24/P03`.
- **Woodbridge/Sekforde work:** the artificial-flower lead gives women and skilled work a possible central role. We now have an archival trail; the workers' tasks, opportunities and constraints need investigation before a scene. `CW-LC-P20`, `CW-CO-S04/S05`.
- **Exmouth today:** choose a revealing current activity rather than praise the street generically. An independent operator, food work or the intriguing historic hot-drinks lamp could provide a lighter finish; the lamp is still an unverified story lead. `CW-LC-P34`, `CW-CO-P01`, `CW-PW-T-voicemap`.

**Two walking subjects to test against the route:** how homes, small workshops and streets fitted together; how the district's daily rhythms changed as work changed. Each needs a developed subject and one or two visible anchors. They must not repeat the surrounding stops. We will place and budget them only after measuring suitable legs.

**Park for this version:** a full Charterhouse or St Bartholomew's interior, the western Hatton Garden circuit and the entire waterworks story. These remain strong material; combining them all would obscure this walk's centre.

## B: a different combination of the same pool

Build around the **Green's public life**, one **livelihood/assistance story around Woodbridge**, **Finsbury Health Centre**, **Spa Fields/Exmouth as places of recreation and everyday use**, and the **waterworks / Quentin Blake Centre** relationship if the northern route earns its extra distance. The savings-bank discovery is an alternative to the work/assistance story, not another institution to append automatically.

The walking subjects could explore the practical conditions of everyday life between public buildings, and how leisure or shared provision changed what people could do with a day. The chapter and stop allocations must prevent repetition. Source clusters: `CW-LC-P07/P13/P20/P24/P25/P28/P29/P34`, plus the current NHS and archival checks in [GAP-CHECK](GAP-CHECK.md).

The ending would need an earned connection between past provision and present activity. The new illustration centre is a useful lead, not automatic proof that cultural redevelopment benefits everyone or that an interior visit fits this tour. Smithfield could become an opening reference or be omitted; forcing it into this northern combination would need a route reason.

## What the choice sets in motion

Select the more appealing experience, or identify particular material you want retained. Then researcher, route assembler and writer can work from a concrete brief: investigate the strongest candidate questions, compare feasible sequences, identify the two walking legs, and produce a short outline before full prose. A discovered better story may change the provisional promise.

Exact distance, duration, transport endpoints and safe listening positions come from that work. The existing North Finchley map also needs a bounded area extension before a new package can run independently. No phone time is needed to choose between these directions or complete the next desk stage.
