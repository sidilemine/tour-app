## Candidate clusters and assembly choices

These are editorial inferences from the catalogue, not selected themes or measured routes.

**Compact southern/central portrait:** consider Smithfield, lower St John Street, the Gate and Green. Food work, trade, print and changing building uses could supply variety. This pool has several overlapping published treatments; selecting different insights matters more than adding more famous names.

**A broader portrait of everyday provision and pleasure:** consider the Green/Close, institutional streets, Spa Fields/Exmouth and the waterworks edge.
