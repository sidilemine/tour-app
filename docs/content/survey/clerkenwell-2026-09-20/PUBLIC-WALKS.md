## Coverage and limits

Ten works/originators across the assigned families: **three guided operators, four self-guided/audio works and three books**. The JSON has **11 tour/resource records, 13 source records and 63 provisional places**: the OS book contributes two area routes. These totals do not imply 11 fully inspected itineraries. The source-family ceiling is respected; related product pages, samples and maps belong to the same work.

Actual public pages were read. The Metro map was downloaded, extracted and visually inspected; all eight pages of the image-only OS sample were inspected. The *Rebel Footprints* preview was read through web text extraction, with page boundaries; its local chapter was unavailable. No book or tour was bought, audio played, guide contacted or route walked. Temporary publisher files are outside the deliverables.

Coordinates are deliberately absent. Place matches and candidate angles support discovery; they establish neither history nor a safe standing position. Source prose, map labels and marketing omissions retain separate statuses. History From Below’s Aru walk was opened in both lanes, then left to [local-context.json](local-context.json), **CW-LC-T07 / CW-LC-S06**, for a single extraction. Civic/community and current-venue work belongs to that handoff.

## Recurrence, alternatives and possible assembly directions

**Recurring within this inspected pool:** St John’s, the Green, Smithfield and Charterhouse dominate the overlapping routes. For example, the Jones, My London Walks, Lady and Metro resources all name St John’s Gate, Smithfield and Charterhouse. This is evidence of repeated inclusion, not independent historical corroboration or measured popularity. The OS contents-only records contribute no stop-frequency evidence. The marketed but unnamed London Walks buildings are not silently matched to named monuments.

**Less repeated in this pool:** the gin reliefs are explicit in Jones; the Exmouth lamp is explicit in the VoiceMap sample; the former savings bank and the Hope’s workday rhythm appear in My London Walks; St. John’s food culture receives attention in Lady’s walk. These are discovery leads, not originality claims. Metro’s snack-bar and pub labels establish inclusion only; they do not supply stories or current trading status.

Two provisional directions could combine this pool with the local-context research:

1. **How the district works:** market trade, small manufacture, publishing, water and everyday services. A walking chapter could investigate the connected railway/river landscape; another could follow changing working and domestic routines toward Exmouth. Check the particular mechanisms and visible anchors before deciding a route.
2. **Who gets space in the city:** religious estates, housing, public speech and institutions. A possible walking chapter could investigate how people lived and worked between the landmarks; another could connect political gathering to streets and squares. Balance conflict with an everyday or humorous discovery rather than borrowing another guide’s dramatic sequence.

These are choices for Sidi, **not selected themes, routes or narration**. Neither requires absorbing every northern and western extension. Angel, Hatton Garden and St Paul’s approaches remain nearby comparisons. This lane supplies limited New River Head coverage; the local-context pool should resolve that boundary before assembly.

## Three consequential follow-ups

1. **Evidence behind the promising small stories.** Check the Exmouth lamp, gin reliefs and worker routines against period or institutional sources before writing. An entertaining listing is insufficient support.
2. **Present-day fit and access.** Reconcile market redevelopment and old business names with the local-context official checks; verify only selected interiors, passages and approaches. Public directions are not access evidence.
3. **Book depth, if it changes selection.** The withheld Clerkenwell chapters could add interpretation, but broad discovery already supports options. Consult a legitimately available chapter only if assembly exposes a useful unresolved question; no purchase is required to finish this handoff.
