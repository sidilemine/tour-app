# Commercial and public-platform tour survey: Finchley

Research date: 17 September 2026. Read with the [survey protocol](../AREA-TOUR-SURVEY.md) and the other survey source families. This is discovery research; it does not verify a provider’s historical claims, route safety or current availability. [Structured catalogue](commercial.json) retains the published places, partial order, story angles, metadata, variants, limitations and search log.

## What changes our understanding

- The strongest commercial coverage clusters around East Finchley, Fortis Green and Muswell Hill. A product sold under **North Finchley** is actually an East Finchley-based Kinks route with a bus segment to Konk. The reseller category cannot establish a useful route around our bus station.
- Finchley’s published themes are substantially broader than musicians: suburban development and conservation, working institutions, woods/water, literature and wartime civilian life. These are useful alternative lenses for the same area.
- Explicit local precedents for making ordinary streets interesting include architectural change, institutional life and literary readings. Our original scripts should use checked underlying evidence and their own structure.
- The direct North Finchley literary walk and the unpublished Natelson itinerary are valuable new leads. Their existence does not justify filling missing stop lists with guesses.

## Priority implications for our tours

1. Retain the bus-station convenience constraint and distinguish nearby comparisons from reachable additions. There is no evidence here that copying an East Finchley commercial route would improve the two North Finchley loops.
2. Use urban change as the connective research question for Tally Ho, cinemas, shopping and cultural/community institutions. It supplies a potential larger thread without forcing every story into a musician biography.
3. Treat Finchley in Fiction as a source of literary leads, especially for a future green/literary variant. The long event and refreshment stops do not demonstrate that every reading had a strong visible anchor.
4. Do not revive the rejected minor memorial stop merely because a wartime tour exists. Owner preference remains active; retain that route as a contrasting theme in the catalogue.
5. Access, geography and underlying claims still need independent checking for adopted material. For example, the commercial dancehall/song association is a research question, not corroboration of an exact venue.

## Coverage and gaps

This branch covered 16 route/visit records plus six additional leads. Counts include partial descriptions and one estate visit; they are not 16 complete scripts or 16 independent local loops. Same-operator variants and resellers are explicitly linked. The full area survey must combine the community/heritage and specialist branches, then deduplicate shared tours.

Targeted searches covered North Finchley, Finchley Central/Church End, East Finchley, Woodside Park, Fortis Green and Muswell Hill; operators, event archives, platforms and video descriptions. The exact query list is in JSON. No Finchley-specific VoiceMap or GPSmyCity product surfaced; izi.TRAVEL supplied an adjacent Parkland sound walk. Go Jauntly supplied concrete local green-route previews. This records public search coverage, not absence of unindexed/app-only products.

Paid scripts and booklets, private itineraries and unexposed app steps remain unknown. Search results and direct pages occasionally differed in dates/availability; dated records are historical unless explicitly stated. A published duration is not our own timing evidence. One web route’s header endpoint conflicts with its final listed point; this is retained rather than silently corrected.

Further leads: the Natelson June 2000 North Finchley/Friern Barnet itinerary; Temple Fortune–East Finchley booklet; Marilyn Greene’s inter-war architecture walk; bounded venue visits; public video descriptions. These gaps need pursuing only if a corresponding candidate would change our two tours. They do not require buying competitors’ tours before scripting.

No purchase, contact, booking, media copying or physical test was performed. Structured records parsed successfully; no app behavior changed.
