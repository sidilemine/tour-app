# Finchley specialist walking-tour survey

Research tranche, 17 September 2026. The [structured catalogue](thematic.json) holds 16 records, published stop/story mappings, unknowns, five targeted fact checks and the actual query log. Merge by original URL and originator with the other survey tranches before counting area-wide tours. This is public research, not experience of taking these walks.

## Most useful findings for A and B

**Keep A's North Finchley focus and make its entertainment thread develop.** Nearby Kinks walks connect biography, work and place; they are principally East Finchley/Muswell Hill comparisons. Even a reseller labelled “North Finchley” starts elsewhere. The useful local connection is Grand Arcade, already selected by Sidi. Introduce what the visitor can see, then the musicians, shops and gathering places that made the centre useful. Treat the detailed public itineraries as discovery material; write independently. [Brit Music Tours](https://britmusictours.com/tour/the-kinks-north-london-walking-tour/), [London Walks](https://www.walks.com/our-walks/the-kinks-in-london/).

**B has a particularly relevant contemporary comparison.** Grange Big Local's East Finchley trail uses ordinary community projects as its subjects. Our inference is that B can earn its neighbourhood theme through particular choices, work and visible results. This is a design comparison, not evidence Sidi enjoys community stories. It does not justify substituting distant stops into the bus-station route. [Grange Big Local Trail](https://www.barnet.gov.uk/events/grange-big-local-trail).

**The Elephant can carry the estate story forward, but its best physical details are indoors.** The local heritage officer dates the earlier tavern to 1870 and the present building to 1927; CAMRA identifies surviving interior features alongside later changes. Keep an outdoor story modest and accurate, with an optional interior observation only if convenient. Do not claim an uninterrupted original interior or invent why the pub was renamed. [Local heritage history](https://northfinchleytowncentre.co.uk/local-stories/story/pubs-inns-and-alehouses/), [CAMRA inventory](https://camra.org.uk/pubs/elephant-inn-north-finchley-157939).

**Make the walking chapter specific to this area's development.** Architecture walks offer a useful technique: explain a development idea, then let listeners recognise its consequences in materials, windows, streets and building groups. Moss Hall requires its own evidence; the social programme of Hampstead Garden Suburb must not migrate into its story. [C20 Society walk](https://secure.c20society.org.uk/Default.aspx?EventId=858&tabid=93), [Art Deco Society report](https://www.artdecosociety.uk/blouses-skirts-and-twitterns-the-hampstead-garden-suburb/).

**Do not restore rejected solemn or lengthy detours just because another tour uses that subject.** The war and literary walks establish a richer local repertoire for future tours. Their public descriptions do not reveal enough to fill missing stop lists. Sidi's rejection of the current memorial and Stanhope detours remains in force. [War walk](https://barnetpost.co.uk/2025/08/20/finchley-goes-to-war-guided-walk-on-sunday-7th-september/), [2014 literary announcement](https://greenacrewriters.blogspot.com/2014/04/a-literary-finchley-walk.html).

## Corrections and source handling

- A local-listing campaign is not a guarantee of permanent preservation. A walking blog implies the Arcade was saved; the local-democracy report explicitly describes the limit of the protection. Keep any final current-development claim tied to a dated council decision. [2022 report](https://barnetpost.co.uk/2022/12/19/the-grand-arcade-added-to-heritage-list-after-successful-campaign/).
- The Kinks fan map distinguishes current school premises from the site attended by the brothers. Preserve this kind of historical-place distinction; a modern institutional address alone is inadequate. [Public place directory](https://london.kastoffkinks.co.uk/html/muswell.html).
- The 2014 literary guide gives Barnet-wide examples while withholding his walk's authors. They are not Finchley tour stops. The 2016 festival schedule confirms actual named break locations, while its reading locations remain unknown. [2016 programme](https://greenacrewriters.blogspot.com/2016/06/finchley-literary-festival.html).
- Tonero explicitly describes researching existing walks before connecting and physically revising his own routes. This is a relevant practitioner precedent for the owner's survey-first direction, not a controlled finding about tour quality. [Method](https://tonero.me.uk/walksaboutmob.htm).

## Coverage and stopping point

Queries cover music/Kinks, literary festivals, architecture/Open House, religious history, social history, Woodside Park and walking blogs/videos. Results repeatedly lead to the same identifiable operators and local resources. No dedicated Finchley faith walk with a published stop itinerary was found in this tranche; matches commonly concern events elsewhere or Finchley Road. That is a search gap, not a statement that no such walk exists.

Videos were discovered but not watched; titles and generic descriptions cannot establish their routes or present conditions. Historic event listings, private interiors, school grounds and garden opening arrangements remain dated evidence. Direct retrieval failed for the Grange site and some event/video pages; the JSON distinguishes indexed text from fetched pages.

The survey now supplies enough thematic comparison to revise A/B and write their scripts. Further distant tour research is unlikely to change the immediate North Finchley choice. Current standing places, crossings and narration claims still need the separate route/claim checks; this catalogue does not certify them.
