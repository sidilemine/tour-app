## What seems worth researching next

These are researcher judgements, not selected stories or observed enjoyment.

### The village is more than a collection of famous addresses

The strongest source leads explain visible arrangements: two households behind Keats House's single-looking facade; three homes behind Goldfinger's modernist frontage; private gardens combined into Waterlow's estate; charitable land whose income still helps residents. They give a listener something that being here can make intelligible. [Keats House history](https://www.cityoflondon.gov.uk/things-to-do/attractions-museums-entertainment/keats-house/history-of-the-house), [Goldfinger construction](https://www.nationaltrust.org.uk/visit/london/2-willow-road/the-construction-of-2-willow-road), [Waterlow history](https://www.waterlowpark.org.uk/the-park/history/), [Wells and Campden Trust](https://hwct.org.uk/about-us/).

For Goldfinger, the most distinctive additional material is the wartime art exhibition in the family home. That could make modernity and social conviction tangible, if chosen; it should not become a second complete biography. The Trust's account is an underlying research lead, not our permission to invent a gathering or dialogue. [Custodian account](https://www.nationaltrust.org.uk/visit/london/2-willow-road/history-of-hampstead-and-2-willow-road).

### Keep the present specific

The Wells and Campden Trust's continuing grants and Keats Community Library's volunteer-supported service connect local history to ordinary life now. At Waterlow, distinguish the Camden-managed park from the independently run arts house. This avoids an undefined account of a place being “saved by the community.” [Trust](https://hwct.org.uk/), [library](https://ww2.keatscommunitylibrary.org.uk/), [Camden](https://www.camden.gov.uk/our-historical-parks), [Lauderdale](https://www.lauderdalehouse.org.uk/about-us).

The Heath's conservation history contains more than one argument: preventing building did not settle whether it should then be made into an orderly park. That is a promising tension, but the Society's own campaigning language is a perspective to investigate. Exact legal arrangements and current pond users need focused research. [Society origins](https://www.heathandhampstead.org.uk/home/origins/).

### Strong atmosphere needs a specific source

Timed cloud studies offer a way into Constable's practice; a preserved fair newsreel offers a different Heath from today's quiet stroll. The first is a museum object lead and the second **catalogue metadata only**: no claim is made to have viewed the film or found an exact painting location. [Museum interpretation](https://digital.nga.gov.au/archive/exhibition/constable/detail.cfm%3Firn%3D145017%26bioartistirn%3D18283%26mnuid%3D2%26galid%3D7.html), [BFI catalogue](https://replay.bfi.org.uk/video/7c0e3e74-1d7b-5828-aade-c86d17233cee).

## Source-quality and coverage limits

- Public-tour survey came first. Subsequent venue/history checks enriched leads; they did not retrospectively turn every history page into a walking tour.
- The six-family survey is divided between researchers. This handoff owns civic/heritage/community, specialist archival/custodian and current-place material. Commercial/blog/audio/book work belongs to the complementary handoff.
- This pass followed National Trust, City, museum, society and charity references. The Highgate-start extension is deliberate, bounded and recorded separately in the search log. No paid book, private script, membership login or guide contact was needed.
- Full Northern Heights books, Burgh production text and several original archives remain unavailable/unread. The existing North Hill PDF was sampled rather than mining every peripheral building. No stop coordinates or safe standing areas are invented.
- Present-day everyday commerce is thinner than the institutional coverage. Once the route is selected, one short business/current-use check would be more useful than another broad list of famous residents.
- Two likely traps are flagged: National Trust's 1993 handover versus 1994 acquisition wording for Willow Road, and the distinction between Romney's former assembly house and Holly Bush's former stables/catering wing. Resolve or omit exact detail if selected.
- Generic local SEO pages included contradictory fountain dates and an invitation to taste water from Well Walk. They are excluded as factual support. A result rank is not evidence of local expertise.
- Lauderdale's own fuller Nell Gwyn account labels both residence and the famous window incident uncertain. Do not turn it into confident historical colour merely because an institutional summary sounds firmer. [Explicit uncertainty](https://www.lauderdalehouse.org.uk/about-us/our-history/nell-gwynn).
