# Highgate, the Heath and Hampstead — public-tour survey

**21 September 2026. Broad discovery complete before our own selection.** Sidi requested a second complete tour, then selected **Highgate Underground station** as its start. The original Hampstead loop is superseded; it remains useful comparison material.

## Catalogue and coverage

The [consolidated index](catalogue.json) retains 67 source records from 66 distinct URLs, 38 walk/resource records and 171 raw place records. Twenty-three clear cross-handoff identities are merged into **148 discovery entries**. These counts include nearby comparisons, partial guided-tour descriptions, book indexes and on-site programmes. They do not mean 38 complete itineraries or 148 approved destinations.

The index records the exact handoff hashes and maps every raw place once. It deliberately preserves composite labels such as “Well Walk / Burgh House”, a building versus its grounds, and a tomb versus its churchyard. The duplicated Open House page is one source. Repeated programmes, reseller descriptions and the same author's two products are not independent historical corroboration.

The six source families received a bounded sweep, followed by the requested Highgate extension. Paid audio scripts, unavailable book chapters and some maps remain unread. Published stops and story angles are discovery material: no third-party script, audio or imagery is copied into our tour.

## What the survey changes

**A crossing needs selective endpoints.** Joining two complete village tours would produce a much longer outing. Existing Highgate–Hampstead walks can run to 15.5 km; their titles alone conceal the scope. Kenwood, Parliament Hill, the Pergola and upper Hampstead are alternatives, not compulsory additions.

**The visible arrangements offer better starting points than a celebrity list.** Made reservoirs, a modern home that became heritage, and two households behind Keats House's facade can connect human activity to what the visitor sees. A primary print of Highgate's mock tavern oath adds a different, lighter register during focused research.

**A quiet landscape still has a present.** Conservation, swimming and community institutions offer current context. Research must identify actual actors and mechanisms, rather than saying vaguely that “the community” saved or maintains a place.

**Monday changes the route.** The intended outing must work from public paths and exteriors. Fenton, Willow Road, Keats House and Burgh House cannot supply required interiors today. The Pergola also has a current maintenance notice; a western detour is particularly hard to justify.
