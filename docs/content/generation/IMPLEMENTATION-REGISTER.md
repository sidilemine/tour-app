## Accepted constraints

- London is the first generation test city. Current app implementation remains walking first; other modes and global coverage require their own scope and evidence.
- The Planner owns the brief, geographic interpretation and consequential user conversation. The Producer owns acceptance and the finished experience. Staffing may combine responsibilities, but must preserve their obligations.
- Default planning combines concise intake, bounded discovery and an informed concept discussion. A precise request or explicit delegation can simplify this cycle.
- Familiarity, famous versus less familiar places, commentary depth and desired discovery are separate brief dimensions. Preserve essential requirements, preferences, delegations and assumptions separately.
- Preserve audience focus through planning, research, selection and writing. For each selected stop, pass on why it matters to this user, what they are likely to recognise or know already, what the tour can help them notice or understand beyond that, and its connection to the present location or surroundings. Audience assumptions are explicit and revisable judgments. Description and evocation earn attention through orientation, noticing or interpretation; generic familiar scenes must not displace the distinctive supported material.
- Use qualitative editorial judgment about airtime. Unexpected material, significance, human interest, noticing and pleasure can each justify inclusion. A hook should lead to a worthwhile contribution; material earns time in proportion to that contribution, audience fit and competing material. These are possible reasons, not a required structure, mandatory hook at every stop or numerical scoring rule. Calibrate with contrasting evidence grounded samples and listening feedback.
- Research, route design and remote inspection develop together. The generation team has no routine field team. Remote observations and simulation must retain their limits.
- Begin with broad source entry points. Current candidates are Google Places or a comparable geographic index, London Archives, London Museum, Layers of London and Atlas Obscura. Local specialists such as Spitalfields Life enter targeted research. No assignment must exhaust the directory.
- Claims retain source locations, supporting passages, qualifications and review status. History, physical observations and changing practical information have separate evidence needs. Essential uncertainty requires evidence, redesign or a justified change to the experience.
- Specialists write assigned findings; the Producer controls consequential acceptance. Reviews identify the checked version and reopen only affected work unless the change warrants broader reassessment.
- Route design combines agent selection and narrative judgment with routing tools for connected geometry and journey estimates. Stop count varies with the brief. An explicit timeline counts overlapping activity once, preserves practical time and silence, and distinguishes approximate duration from a hard finish time. Substantial stories at stops and selective short passages on simple legs are the accepted starting default; their calibration remains open.
- The Scout inspects actual imagery with map and source context, separates camera and visitor positions, and preserves observations, interpretations, unknowns and dates. Use early checks of consequential dependencies, coverage of the selected route and targeted rechecks after changes. The Scout may propose improvements; essential uncertainty needs evidence or a change to the experience. Tool reliability, coverage and calibration remain implementation work.
- Generation is separate from playback. Downloaded planned tours use local maps, directions, narration and recovery. Preserve manual controls, persistent pause and the existing player contract.
- Prepare temporary assets and simulate before expensive final production. Check measured final audio duration. Designing automation does not authorize a backend, paid usage, a reusable city knowledge base, driving or arbitrary offline rerouting.

## Status and timing

**Open** means an implementation choice remains unresolved. **Proposed** means a recommendation awaits discussion or evidence. **Accepted design** means the owner has accepted the operating principle; implementation choices and empirical calibration can remain open. **Deferred** means its function design or expansion comes later. Use **Selected for prototype**, **Validated**, **Blocked** or **Superseded** as work progresses; acceptance or selection is not a passed test.

“Before” indicates when the choice affects work, not an instruction to stop all independent work. Domain responsibilities identify the function that should help resolve a choice; Codex owns engineering evidence and the Producer coordinates dependencies.

## Proposed connecting protocol

Status: Proposed on 6 October 2026. This elaborates accepted shared record and lifecycle principles; exact fields, transitions and assignment criteria remain open under G02 to G06. Begin with the common handoff and initial planning cycle, then specify the research, route and scouting loop, editorial and production handoffs, and revision or failure behavior.

A task should state its purpose and the next decision it supports; responsible function; scope; relevant brief and input record versions; required audience context; allowed choices and limits; tool and effort budget; expected outputs with evidence or unresolved questions; and completion and stopping conditions. The shared record is authoritative. A handoff gives a concise explanation and references to actual records and evidence, rather than relying on a summary as the whole evidence pack. Preserve interest rationale, assumed audience baseline, intended discovery and present place connection in relevant downstream tasks.

Distinguish task execution from acceptance of its output. Proposed task states are queued, working, returned, blocked and cancelled; outputs and reviews separately identify proposed, accepted or needs revision status and the version assessed. Returning a task is not an assurance that its decision goal has been satisfied. Record useful partial findings, unsuccessful searches, missing dependencies and recommended next action. Any remaining obligation keeps an owner. The designated function lead or reviewer can assess routine work within delegated authority; the Producer controls consequential acceptance and lifecycle commitments. Avoid an extra Producer approval for every draft record or routine finding.

Dependent work can start when its own inputs are usable. An uncertain entrance blocks directions relying on it, while unrelated historical research may continue. Proposed work can support clearly marked exploration or samples. Only appropriately accepted material supplies later commitments and release. Version changes flag affected dependencies; retain an older task's useful findings but check compatibility before using them in the current decision. A source failure, exhausted budget or blocked connection returns evidence and a concrete recovery or redesign option, without silently relaxing essential requirements.

Proposed initial cycle: the Planner extracts the request into a provisional brief, distinguishing supplied requirements, preferences, defaults, delegation and unresolved questions. Ask for missing information when it materially changes the next decision and cannot be responsibly resolved within delegation. The Producer checks that bounded discovery has a clear goal and sufficient scope; the Research Lead returns a concise set of viable experience directions, supporting leads, consequential uncertainties and useful negative findings. These are concepts, not promises of a final route. The Planner presents a recommendation and only materially different alternatives when helpful, or proceeds under explicit delegation. The Producer records brief acceptance and assigns deeper research, initial route work and early scouting of consequential dependencies. Detailed facts and physical dependencies may remain open with owners; an accepted brief establishes enough direction to commission generation.

Prototype this protocol against a sparse neighbourhood request, fixed endpoints with a hard finish time, explicit delegation, an unsuccessful discovery pass, an older task return after a brief change and a newly closed key connection. Record whether assignments are actionable, questions are consequential, useful work is retained, required constraints survive and the next decision is clear. Relevant existing tests are T01 to T05, T08, T14 and T22; no result has been recorded.

## Full process draft and bounded effort experiments

[Tour_generation_full_process_draft readable copy](handoff/Tour_generation_full_process_draft.readable.txt) proposes the complete connected process S00 to S10, detailed handoffs, readiness criteria, revision and failure behavior, and a supervised comparison plan. It expands the accepted workflow without replacing it. The new connecting details, numerical limits and wider editorial operating proposals remain proposed; drafting does not accept or validate them.

All profiles preserve the brief, supported factual and physical assertions, essential route and access requirements, worthwhile audience interpretation, listening comprehension, consistent final assets and required offline playback behavior. Lean reduces optional discovery, comparison and refinement; it does not reduce assertion review or coverage of selected stops and legs. Balanced is the recommended initial hypothesis, with extra effort focused on consequential weaknesses. Deep is bounded wider exploration and refinement, not proof of an ideal outdoor experience.

Initial experimental settings for comparable 60 to 90 minute London requests: screen at most 8, 16 or 24 distinct candidates; allow 1, 2 or 3 targeted research rounds after the initial passes; cap substantive route proposals at 2, 3 or 5 including the first; allow 1, 2 or 3 batched content correction passes after initial review; and allow 1, 1 or 2 corrective renders per affected final clip. Candidate limits do not set stop count. Split tasks and renamed issues do not reset allowances. Proposed job cutoffs are 10, 20 and 40 minutes, with total cost ceilings C, 2C and 4C. These are experimental cutoffs, not measured performance or price claims. C must be defined from actual provider costs and an authorized test budget before paid trials.

Proposed stopping controls: each followup names the open question, a changed tactic and completion condition. Stop for Producer arbitration after two consecutive substantive attempts neither add decision changing evidence nor resolve a required issue, or sooner when a smaller profile allowance ends. Stop an A to B to A oscillation without new evidence or a requirement change. Freeze ordinary discovery after route acceptance and optional stylistic changes after content acceptance; new blockers still require action. A closed issue needs a relevant input change or new evidence before reopening. Resolve repeated subjective disagreement by Producer judgment; resolve factual and physical disagreements through evidence, supported qualification, removal or permitted redesign. Required uncertainty cannot become approval because effort runs out.

Enforce one total job ledger for incurred cost, outstanding concurrent commitments and conservative estimates of unfinished required work. Test a 25 percent completion reserve, increasing it when remaining mandatory work requires more. Permit at most one safe automatic technical retry for a transient failure within existing limits; check uncertain paid or write outcomes before another attempt. Record waiting, active preparation and total elapsed separately. User changes, task aliases and restarts retain incurred usage and do not silently create fresh budgets. Ready, awaiting a consequential user decision, blocked and cancelled are explicit outcomes; retained partial work stays a draft.

The comparison starts with bounded calibration, then matched profiles on one request, expanding only when findings and the authorized budget justify it. Hold models, tools, source access and voice comparable, separate cold and warm reuse, and record listening order. Assess quality with concrete listener examples alongside readiness, defects, total elapsed time, active work and all charged costs including failed attempts. Report cost per ready tour as well as successful run cost. Existing device and outdoor procedures retain their own evidence scope. See new tests T37 to T39 and existing T01 to T03, T08, T12 to T14, T20 to T22 and T29 to T36.

## Build release and learning

Responsibility: Builder, Tester and Producer. Status: Accepted design on 6 October 2026. The owner accepted the responsibilities, cycle and starting defaults below. Implementation choices and calibration remain open. Existing playback, offline and evidence constraints remain binding; no package, narration or device behavior is recorded here as validated.

Accepted Builder responsibility: assemble the current accepted route, script, directions, evidence links and required assets into a repeatable package compatible with the actual player. Production may normalize formatting and apply approved pronunciation instructions, but changes to meaning, physical guidance, route or the experience return to their owners. Derive the manifest and delivered content from the same versioned record. Keep landmark and visitor positions distinct, retain rights and attribution records, and include all resources needed for the planned offline experience.

Accepted audio production: make it an explicit responsibility within building rather than requiring another permanent agent. Use a short sample to choose delivery and check difficult names, temporary narration for editorial and timing rehearsal, then the approved script for final production. Start with one clear voice and no soundtrack. Check the finished audio against the intended words and meaning, pronunciation, audible completeness, pacing and measured duration. Automated comparisons assist this review; they do not alone establish the quality of what a listener hears. Return a longer or weaker rendition to the relevant Editor, Route Designer or Producer instead of silently removing qualifications or compressing delivery to meet a duration target. Clip boundaries must fit the player's supported sequencing, pause, skip and replay behavior.

Accepted Tester responsibility: review the actual candidate package independently from assembly, using deterministic checks for schema, references, required resources, checksums, review coverage and compatibility, plus journey scenarios and listening checks for integrated behavior. Include early arrival, slow travel, pauses, skips, weak location, offline cold start, failed import and reopening. A changed clip or position can make previously correct text and timing inconsistent. Reuse applicable evidence for unchanged components; record the exact tested package and player versions. Platform and physical phone validation follow the existing roadmap, and desk testing does not establish outdoor experience quality. Repeated full device testing is warranted by relevant changes or failures, not by every ordinary content revision.

Accepted cycle: prepare a provisional package and narration; rehearse and simulate; resolve content and integration issues; produce final audio; measure and review it; validate the final package; let the Producer assess that exact version for delivery. Revisions may return to earlier stages. Rebuild and reassess affected dependencies, reuse valid unchanged work, and repeat deterministic integrity checks on every candidate package. Work and retries remain within the authorized budget; unresolved blockers cannot be converted into acceptance when effort runs out.

Accepted release responsibility: the Producer accepts the finished experience against the brief, current evidence, final assets and test results. Unsupported factual or physical guidance, incompatible or missing required assets, unresolved critical access dependencies, and failure of the required offline or playback behavior block readiness. Optional editorial polish may remain as an explicit tradeoff. The delivery summary gives the intended start, end, duration, distance and relevant access conditions, and states the basis and limits of checks when material to using the tour. Ordinary local delivery does not imply external publication or another compulsory user approval after delegated planning. Refresh consequential changing information for advance tours when appropriate; revise and accept affected content before supplying an update. Active sessions retain their content version rather than silently mixing new assets with old progress. Visitor reports initiate investigation and retain provenance before influencing other tours.
