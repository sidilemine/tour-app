Editorial target: a 35–45 minute daylight walk, roughly 6–8 minutes of final story audio with generous silence. This is a design budget, **not a measured duration or distance**. The present scripts are shorter listening drafts. The stop order, garden loop and last two standing areas may change after routing and inspection. Do not squeeze several triggers into nearby garden points simply to reach six stops; combine or replace them if spacing is poor.

## Research ledger and limits

Public Nominatim responses are retained in [research](../../content/finchley/research/README.md). The station query returned both a station building and a bus stop; only the building result informs the landmark field. The first house query returned nothing; the refined Avenue House query returned multiple places. The Finchley house result was selected by its name/address. None of these coordinates is a safe standing position, entrance or routing waypoint.

The historical content relies on [TfL's design history](https://tfl.gov.uk/corporate/about-tfl/culture-and-heritage/harry-becks-tube-map), [Historic England's church listing](https://historicengland.org.uk/listing/the-list/list-entry/1287095), its linked school assessment and the estate's own historical pages (all linked per claim in the manifest). Source-checked statements still need editorial review. The school report distinguishes an original main building from a later primary building; that distinction must survive field identification.

[Published garden hours](https://www.stephenshouseandgardens.com/visit/opening-hours/opening-hours) retrieved on 13 September list 08:00–18:00, changing to 08:00–16:00 in winter. Recheck before scheduling: the page also has an older event closure notice, and house/Bothy access is separate. Its website-accessibility page is not evidence of physical step-free access. No interior, Bothy entry or particular tree is required by this draft.

No copied photographs, Tube map artwork, paid TTS or map tiles are included. The estate's water-system page was unavailable during research; no claim about how that system worked has been added. Council pages that returned access errors were not treated as verified evidence.

## Field review sheet

Use the [candidate-specific blank worksheet](FINCHLEY-FIELD-WORKSHEET.md) for the actual observations and per-leg timing.

For each candidate record a date and reviewer, landmark identity, visitor coordinate, approach path, actual facing/viewpoint, crossing/obstruction conditions, surfaces/steps/gradient, hours/gates, permitted access and a fallback. Keep private raw observations in ignored `local-data/`; promote only reviewed public tour information to the manifest.

Then route between **visitor points** using a real pedestrian router, preserve its geometry/maneuvers and provenance, review every leg, and time the walk. Reserve audio windows for directions; an actionable cue can pause narration and resume it only if no manual pause intervenes. This draft does not add a navigation engine to M1.

Compare the baseline with [two supervised experiment briefs](E1-BRIEFS.md). A positive listening response alone is not E1 completion: the variants still need feasible routed plans, reviewed orientation and actual walking feedback.
